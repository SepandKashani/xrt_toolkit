XRT Toolkit¶

XRT Toolkit (XTK) is a collection of utilities to compute X-Ray Transforms.

Rationale¶

Tomography measures line integrals of a function \(f: \bR^{D} \to \bR\), \(D \in \{2, 3\}\):

(1)¶\[\xrt[f](\bbn, \bbt) = \int_{\bR} f(\bbt + \alpha \bbn) \, d\alpha,\]

along rays with anchors \(\bbt \in \bR^{D}\) and unit directions \(\bbn \in \bR^{D}\). Expanding \(f\) in shifts of a basis function \(\psi\) on a uniform lattice

\[f(\bbx) = \sum_{\bbq} f_{\bbq} \, \psi(\bbx - \bbx_{\bbq})\]

makes (1) linear in the coefficients \(f_{\bbq}\):

\[\xrt[f](\bbn, \bbt) = \sum_{\bbq} f_{\bbq} \, \xrt[\psi](\bbn, \bbt - \bbx_{\bbq}).\]

XTK evaluates these sums along arbitrary rays, exactly, for voxels [1, 7] and for higher-order box splines and B-splines [2, 4, 5]. It computes, on CPU and GPU via Dr.Jit [6]:

  • the projections and their adjoint;

  • their gradients with respect to the rays, for geometry calibration [3].
